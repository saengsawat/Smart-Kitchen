Reading guide: "Covered" means the behaviour is already in the prototype, a merged ticket, or a written epic. "MVP add" means small enough and central enough that the architect recommends putting it in the first release. "Fast-follow" means built right after the first release. "Later" means after product-market fit evidence, usually because it needs paid data or an LLM. Costs are engineering time; nothing here adds a cash cost unless the row says so.
