What do these mean?

Every value in KitchenSmart wears a tag showing how sure we are. A tag is our confidence in a fact. It is never a safety check by itself.

✓ Fact

Known Fact. Verified against a source we trust, such as a barcode match or something you typed yourself.

≈ Est.

Estimated. A reasonable guess, such as a shelf-life date or a quantity worked out from meals you have logged. Correct it any time, one tap.

AI · confirm

AI interpretation. Read from a receipt or a photo. It stays a proposal until you confirm it. Nothing enters your inventory as fact until you say so.

Allergen

Allergen alert. The only red tag in the app. Always shown with an icon and words, never colour alone. It never claims a food is guaranteed free of anything, only that we found or did not find a match.
