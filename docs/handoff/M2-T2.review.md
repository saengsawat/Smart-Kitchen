## Findings

**F1 (major)** `routes.ts:78-101`, `app.ts:45-57`: both body schemas declare `additionalProperties: false`, but Fastify's bundled AJV runs with `removeAdditional: true`, so unknown properties are stripped, not rejected. Live probe: `{"type":"DISCARD","amuont":"0.25"}` removed the whole item with a 200. The module comment claims the opposite. Fix: `ajv.customOptions.removeAdditional: false`, tests for 400 on unknown properties, correct the comment. `app.ts` is outside the ticket's scope; architect ruling needed.
**F2 (major)** `write-service.ts:369-371`, `quantity-text.ts:40`: a negative `targetAmount` is accepted, plans a decrease larger than the balance and mints an `OVER_CONSUMPTION` clamp for a malformed request, inflating the correction-rate KPI. Fix: reject a negative target before the subtraction; `deltaAmount` stays signed.
**F3 (minor)** `inventory-writes.db.test.ts:561-571`: the "net effect, not face value" undo test does not discriminate (mutation 5 left it green); undo the overshoot row instead (face +4, group net +8).
**F4 (minor)** `write-service.ts:660-676`: an undo whose compensation would overshoot a drained lot answers 200, changes nothing and mints another clamp. Ruling requested.
**F5 (minor)** CONTRIBUTING seed section and the proposed ARCHITECTURE §7 item 4 claim a member of `sk_app` can run the seed; verified false (`permission denied for table households`, `0006` grants `sk_app` only SELECT there). The owner or superuser role that ran the migrations is required.
**F6 (minor)** `write-service.ts` (705 lines) has no test that runs without `DATABASE_URL`; `increaseLot` is exported and untested.
**F7 (minor)** `inventory-writes.db.test.ts:1052-1067`: `SET ROLE`, `UPDATE`, `RESET ROLE` through `pool.query` separately can land on different connections.
**F8 (minor, scope)** `apps/api/src/app.test.ts` edited (3 lines) for the `{read, write}` runner shape; accept.
**F9 (minor)** `correlationLabel` is neither populated nor in the contract; acceptance criterion (c) should be settled by the architect.

## Opinions (non-blocking)

The replay design (re-plan against the item as it stood before the stored rows, let the ledger's own payload comparison decide) is the best thing in the branch and held under five attack shapes. Undo rows should count as corrections in the KPI; F2 and F4 pollute that metric more than undo rows do. `SK_APP_ROLE` imported from `http/tenant-session.ts` by the seed is the second such caller; consolidate. Two different writes racing on one item is proved only by injection; a two-connection concurrency suite is a cheap follow-up. The `LedgerErrorCode` consistency test's compile-time `AssertNever` pair is the right shape.
