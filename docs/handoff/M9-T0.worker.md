## 3. Network-free export

`pnpm --filter mobile export` with `EXPO_OFFLINE=1 EXPO_NO_TELEMETRY=1` under a `--require` shim (non-loopback `net.connect`, `dns.lookup`, `fetch`, `dgram.send` are logged and blocked). The export succeeded for android, ios and web. The shim logged **3 attempts, not zero**: `dns 1.1.1.1`, `dgram 192.168.1.255`, `dgram 169.254.255.255`. All come from `lan-network` (Expo CLI's local-IP detection), which the two flags do not gate. There was no connect to `cdp.expo.dev` and no `fetch`. This matches the M3-T4e review's F9 observation ("only Expo CLI's own probes"); the flags remove the telemetry connection and the export does not fail when the probes are blocked. If the ticket's acceptance "zero attempts" means literally zero, it cannot be met by these flags.
